## 🕹️ Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / S** | Move your rods up and down |
| **Space** | Trap the ball |
| **Hold Left Click** | Charge a power shot while holding the ball |
| **Release Left Click** | Shoot the ball with your charged power |
| **Right Click** | Perform a lofted shot while holding the ball to lob it over defenders |

### ⚡ Master Your Shots

- **Trap the ball:** Use Space to gain control and prepare your next move.
- **Power shot:** Hold Left Click while holding the ball. The longer you charge, the harder the shot flies. Release to fire!
- **Lofted shot:** Right-click while holding the ball to send it over defenders.
