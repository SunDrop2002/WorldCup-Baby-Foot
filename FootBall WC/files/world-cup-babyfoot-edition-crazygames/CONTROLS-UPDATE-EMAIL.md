# Controls update for CrazyGames — World Cup Babyfoot Edition

## Email

**Subject:** Controls update + mobile support — World Cup Babyfoot Edition

Hi,

We've simplified the controls in World Cup Babyfoot Edition and added full touch
support, so the controls text on the game page is now out of date. Since I can't
edit that field myself, could you replace it with the text below?

Two things changed:

- **Passing was removed.** If the current text mentions Q / E to pass between
  rods, that mechanic no longer exists. The ball now leaves your grip only via a
  shot or the hold timer.
- **Trapping is now a single press.** Space arms a catch rather than being held
  down, and the ball stays latched until you shoot.

The game also now has on-screen touch controls and plays properly on phones, so
I've included a mobile section.

**New controls text:**

> **Desktop**
> W / S — Move your rods up and down
> Space — Trap the ball
> Left click — Kick a loose ball
> Hold left click — Power shot while holding the ball; the longer you hold, the harder it flies. Release to fire.
> Right click — Lofted shot while holding the ball, to lob it over defenders
>
> **Mobile**
> Joystick (left) — Move your rods up and down
> TRAP — Trap the ball
> SHOOT — Tap to kick a loose ball, or hold to charge a power shot and release to fire
> CHIP — Lofted shot, to lob it over defenders

Two related requests while you're in there:

1. Could you **enable the game for mobile**? It now supports touch input
   throughout.
2. Could you set the **supported orientation to landscape only**? The pitch is a
   fixed landscape layout, so portrait isn't playable.

Thanks very much,
[your name]
